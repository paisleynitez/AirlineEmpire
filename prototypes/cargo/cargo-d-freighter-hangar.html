Concept D — Freighter Hangar. Dedicated freighters become real roster entries with payload (tonnes) and volume in place of seats, bought here or in the Buy Aircraft window under a new Freighter band. The right rail is the conversion programme: eligible passenger jets you already own can be sent for a passenger-to-freighter conversion for a fee and a few months out of service, returning as a BCF/P2F with its own hero card. Belly capacity from the passenger fleet is shown so you can see how much freight rides for free before buying a freighter.
