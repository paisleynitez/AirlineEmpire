Concept C — Freight Market. The "opportunities": a live demand map of the world's freight corridors. Every row shows how many tonnes a month a corridor generates, how much of it you already carry (solid) versus what is going unsold (hatched), the spot yield, the quarterly trend and the commodity mix. Events at the top push demand around the map for a limited time. Selecting a corridor produces a launch plan: the freighter that fits, tonnes you would capture, monthly net and the set-up cost, with one click to open the lane.
