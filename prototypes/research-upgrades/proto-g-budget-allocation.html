Implementation G — no slots and no RP pool. You set a monthly R&D spend (a cash line item, like the crew budget) and split it across five departments with sliders. Each department pays its funding into the next program in its own queue; a program completes when its cost is covered, so more money = sooner. Starving a department pauses it without losing progress. Reads like a real airline's engineering budget and slots straight into the existing Budget modal.
