# QA — Three-Logo Shuffle Picker v1.1.5

1. Install the patch and launch `game/index.html`.
2. Start a new game and reach **Airline Logo**.
3. Confirm exactly three logo cards are visible.
4. Confirm no logo-grid scrollbar appears.
5. Confirm **Random Preview** is absent.
6. Confirm the only logo control is **Shuffle**.
7. Press **Shuffle** repeatedly and confirm each press leaves exactly three cards.
8. Select each card and confirm the selection check/outline moves correctly.
9. Confirm the airline-name dice remains inside the name field, immediately left of the checkmark.
10. Continue to the next setup screen and confirm the selected logo persists.
