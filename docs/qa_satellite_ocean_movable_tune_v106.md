# QA — v1.0.6 Satellite Ocean and Movable Tune Launcher

1. Launch by double-clicking `game/index.html`.
2. Start or continue a game and open the world map.
3. Verify oceans retain satellite texture and bathymetric detail rather than appearing as a flat blue overlay.
4. Inspect the Atlantic, Pacific, Caribbean, Mediterranean, Gulf of Mexico, and larger northern seas for natural color variation.
5. Verify land remains untinted and readable.
6. Drag the `Tune` button to each corner and the center of the screen.
7. Verify it remains inside the viewport and does not open while being dragged.
8. Click the button after moving it and verify the Dev Tuner opens normally.
9. Close the game, reopen it, and verify the button returns to its saved position.
10. Confirm direct local-file launch works and no npm or Git operation is required.
