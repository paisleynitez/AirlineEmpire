# QA — Logo Names Without Numeric Suffixes v1.1.5

1. Launch `game/index.html` directly.
2. Start a new game and reach **Airline Logo**.
3. Confirm exactly nine logo cards appear in a 3 × 3 grid.
4. Confirm no scrollbar appears in the logo picker.
5. Press **Shuffle** at least ten times.
6. Confirm no displayed airline name ends in a number, including `2`, `3`, or any other numeric suffix.
7. Confirm every shuffle still displays nine logo cards.
8. Confirm selecting a logo still highlights it and preserves the entered airline name.
9. Confirm the dice and checkmark remain aligned inside the airline-name field.
